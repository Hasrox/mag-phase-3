MAG safety rubric 1.1

The tagger judges the image, not the joke the template might later make.
reject: sexual content involving minors; sexual violence; graphic real-world gore.
review: identifiable private individuals; slur-bearing text; ambiguous cases.
ok: everything else, including crude humor, fictional violence, and toilet humor.

Content that is illegal to possess is deleted from the store, not merely excluded.
The operator may extend this file before the first tag run. The file hash is stored on the tag run. Changing it after seeing gold scores voids the run.
